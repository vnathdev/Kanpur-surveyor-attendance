Entry log

Hours come from the submitted start and end times. Where an end time falls before its start time or a shift runs past 12 hours, the duration is treated as unverified: it is shown in the log but left out of hour totals and averages, so one mistyped time cannot skew a surveyor's record. Tickets and distance from those entries still count. Ask the surveyor to resubmit, and the numbers correct themselves on the next refresh. A submission may list several wards; those are counted as one visit each, and its tickets and distance stay with the submission rather than being split across them, so the ward table shows visits only.
